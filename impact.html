Nairobi, Kenya

Amara Found Her Voice

At 14, Amara hadn't spoken a word in two years after losing her entire family in a flood. A WorldLight outreach team found her in a community shelter and invited her to join a youth choir. Six months later, she sang her first note in front of 10,000 people at the Nairobi show. Today, she performs internationally and is studying music education.
