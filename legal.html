Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2025

WorldLight Tour Organisation ("WorldLight", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information about you when you use our website, purchase tickets, make donations, or sign up to our newsletter.

1. Who We Are

WorldLight Tour is a registered nonprofit organisation headquartered in London, United Kingdom, with regional offices in Berlin and New York. We are registered under charity number 1234567 with the UK Charity Commission.

Data Controller: WorldLight Tour Organisation, 100 Hope Street, London, EC1A 1BB, United Kingdom
Email: privacy@worldlighttour.org
Data Protection Officer: dpo@worldlighttour.org

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide Directly

  • Name, email address, postal address when signing up for our newsletter or creating an account
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never store card details)
  • Donation amounts and frequency preferences
  • Press accreditation details including employer, role and coverage intent
  • Messages submitted via our contact forms

2.2 Data Collected Automatically

  • IP address and browser type for security and analytics purposes
  • Pages visited, time spent, scroll depth and interaction data via anonymised analytics
  • Cookie data as described in our Cookie Policy

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To process donations and send payment confirmations
  • To send the newsletter you have opted into (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To improve our website and user experience through anonymised analytics
  • To process press accreditation requests
  • To comply with legal obligations including charity reporting requirements
  • To respond to your enquiries

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Consent — for newsletter subscriptions and optional analytics cookies
  • Contractual necessity — for processing donations and ticket purchases
  • Legitimate interests — for website security and fraud prevention
  • Legal obligation — for charity compliance and financial reporting

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with trusted processors under data processing agreements:

  • Stripe — payment processing (their privacy policy applies to payment data)
  • Mailchimp — email newsletter delivery
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Cloudflare — CDN, security and performance
  • Plausible Analytics — privacy-first, cookieless analytics (no personal data shared)

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary: newsletter subscriber data until you unsubscribe; donation records for 7 years to meet charity accounting requirements; website analytics data in anonymised form for up to 2 years.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — opt out of processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — at any time for consent-based processing

To exercise any right, contact us at privacy@worldlighttour.org. We will respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK at ico.org.uk.

8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States (by Stripe and Mailchimp). These transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, regular penetration testing, and strict access controls. All staff with data access undergo annual data protection training.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, why we use them, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to site owners.

Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function. They cannot be switched off. They include:

  • wl_cookie — records your cookie consent choice. Expires after 12 months.
  • stripe_session — maintains your secure session during donation checkout. Session only.

Analytics Cookies (Optional)

We use Plausible Analytics — a privacy-first, GDPR-compliant analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. No consent required.

Marketing Cookies (Optional)

If you accept all cookies, we may set cookies from social media platforms (Instagram, YouTube, Twitter/X) to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns. These can be declined without affecting your use of the site.

How to Control Cookies

You can control cookies via:

  • Our cookie banner when you first visit the site
  • Your browser settings — see your browser's help documentation
  • Third-party opt-out tools at youronlinechoices.com

Note: disabling necessary cookies may affect website functionality.

Accessibility

Last updated: March 1, 2025

WorldLight Tour is committed to making our website accessible to everyone — regardless of disability, device, or technology. We are working to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Accessibility Features

⌨️

Full Keyboard Navigation

All interactive elements are reachable and operable via keyboard alone. Tab order follows logical content flow.

🔊

Screen Reader Support

Semantic HTML, ARIA labels, alt text on all meaningful images, and live regions for dynamic content updates.

🎨

Colour Contrast

All text meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios. Gold on dark background is tested at 4.5:1 minimum.

📱

Responsive Design

The site is fully functional on all screen sizes, from mobile to ultra-wide displays, with fluid typography.

🌍

Multilingual Support

The site is available in English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Japanese, Mandarin, Swahili, Hindi, Korean and Ukrainian.

🎬

Captions & Transcripts

All video content includes closed captions in multiple languages. Audio descriptions available on request.

⚡

Reduced Motion

Animations respect the prefers-reduced-motion media query. All parallax and particle effects are disabled for users who prefer reduced motion.

🔡

Resizable Text

Text can be resized up to 200% without loss of content or functionality. We use relative units (rem/em) throughout.

Known Issues

We are aware that the interactive SVG world map has limited screen reader support. We are working on a fully accessible text-based alternative. Please contact us if you need tour date information in an accessible format.

Feedback & Contact

We welcome your accessibility feedback. If you experience any barriers or have suggestions, please contact us:

Accessibility Team

accessibility@worldlighttour.org

We aim to respond to all accessibility enquiries within 5 business days.

Conformance Status

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. "Partially conformant" means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard. A full audit will be published by June 2025.
