Ambassador Program

Spread the WorldLight mission in your community. Three tiers — from local advocate to global ambassador.

Tier 1

Community Advocate

Share WorldLight in your local community, school or workplace. Host a viewing party for the global livestream.

  • Sign up via newsletter
  • Share on social media
  • Receive exclusive Ambassador Kit

Tier 2

City Champion

Organise local events, coordinate volunteer groups, and help promote WorldLight shows in your city.

  • Minimum 3 community events
  • 100+ people reached locally
  • VIP access to your city's show

Tier 3

Global Ambassador

Represent WorldLight at international level. Appear on stage, give speeches and attend the Grand Finale as an official delegate.

  • Application & selection by committee
  • 10,000+ people reached
  • Attendance at Grand Finale
  • Official title & credentials
